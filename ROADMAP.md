## Phase 2: The Testnet ($BANKR)
- **Currency:** $BANKR (Precursor Token)
- **Goal:** Stress test agent interactions without real value at risk.
- **Mechanics:**

    - High velocity trading.
    - "The Countdown": A visualized timer to the end of the $BANKR era.

- **Launch:** Post-countdown.
- **Mechanics:**
