# Enterprise original chain fee coverage

HTTP 1.107 adds private `organization_chain_fees` observations to administrator routing health, hourly monitoring, the protected operator preflight and the operations console. Only version-2 L1/Sepolia and version-3 Tempo original gross uses are included. Revoked and expired grants retain their costs. The view reads original payment receipts and all original treasury payout/refund attempts; a failed on-chain attempt can have a real charged fee without proving settlement completion.

Buyer and treasury roles remain separate. Fixed groups report Ethereum L1 ETH wei, Sepolia ETH wei and Tempo pathUSD base units. Arithmetic and totals use integer strings; decimal display is exact to 18 or six places. The view never adds currencies, converts native gas to dollars or substitutes a reviewed fee ceiling for actual cost.

Each role reports original record count, measured, missing and invalid counts, its known valid fee subtotal, and coverage. `measured` has complete supported stored observations and an actual total. `partial` keeps known fees but leaves the actual total null. `no_records` also leaves the total null. `invalid` excludes contradictory records from its known subtotal and leaves the actual total null. Historical or unsupported null observations are not invariant alerts and are never backfilled by this inspector. Original claims still awaiting payment proof remain covered by the separate enterprise obligation monitor.

The shared strict fee codec binds recorded fee model, chain, original transaction hash and payer; it preserves existing Ethereum/blob arithmetic and Tempo receipt/log ceil conversion. Each observation must also match its original account/use/order linkage and frozen payment chain/token/buyer-or-treasury. Supported Tempo remains sender-paid pathUSD. Buyer fees must fit the original reviewed per-payment ceiling; treasury charges are treasury costs and do not consume or masquerade as buyer fees.

Changed original identity, model, fee arithmetic, buyer fee ceiling or premature fee evidence emits fixed critical `ORGANIZATION_CHAIN_FEE_INVARIANT`. Output contains only fixed group labels, counts, integer fee totals, coverage and timestamps. It exposes no account/order/trade IDs, wallets, hashes, credentials, raw payment terms or private work. The inspector performs one bounded read with a 10,001-record sentinel; more than 10,000 original records or unavailable storage fails private inspection closed, rather than returning a truncated total. Larger histories need a separately designed aggregation strategy.

There is no RPC read, estimate, send, record rewrite or financial transition. Routing admission and enterprise authority remain independently enforced. Missing observations do not authorize fresh funding or replacement refunds. Apply existing migrations through ID 62 before this code; no new migration is needed.
