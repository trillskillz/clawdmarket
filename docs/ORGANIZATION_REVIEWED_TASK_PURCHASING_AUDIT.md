## Original economic identity and permission

The original owner proposes one accepted bid/workspace and the selected independent
current reviewer approves exact frozen task, bidder, buyer, department, quote,
requirements, payment and fee bounds. Pending reviews block generic task funding.
Approved buyer checkout commits one original task trade, original workspace link,
organization attribution and task use atomically. Cancellation and revocation
recover that same trade; task authority creates no service order, route funding
step or workflow child. Other scopes cannot inspect or spend with this authority.

Separately versioned EVM/Tempo intents and exact-byte claims share wallet and
consumed-nonce exclusions with original route/spending claims. Current owner,
reviewer, department, bid, quote, provider and payment scope are checked before
fresh commitments. Original submitted bytes and verified proofs retain their
historical identity for payment/refund recovery after permission closes. Gross
and same-currency fee history joins reviewed task/route/workflow uses once and
cannot be recycled through cancellation or refund.

Original task delivery retains frozen requirements, encrypted structured output,
private artifacts and the original content hash. There is no automatic confirmation
timer for reviewed task work. The actual buyer must explicitly accept the exact
delivery hash before authoritative existing settlement can complete the task.
Seller and buyer journals survive separate process deaths without replacement
checkouts, payment bytes, computation, delivery or payout.
